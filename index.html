How we stay honest

Our commitments

  • Real students only

    In the live campaign, every student and partner featured agrees to appear. No stock photos, no invented testimonials. This mock-up uses stock photos, clearly labelled.

  • Proof before promises

    Each claim on this page is backed by an indicator. Limits and unfinished work are published too.

  • Local and low-cost

    Missions run in the Aube, within existing courses and work-study partnerships. Free for partners.

  • No promotion of alcohol

    Missions with winegrowers focus on farming, heritage and tourism, in line with the French Loi Évin.
